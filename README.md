# MUSA 5500 Final Project: Late-Night Transport Accessibility and Singapore’s Nightlife Geography

**Live Website:** https://chlauryn.github.io/MUSA-5500-Geospatial-Data-Science-Python/

## Overview
Singapore’s nightlife industry faces growing pressures from high operating costs and a mismatch between venue operating hours and public transit schedules. With MRT and regular bus services winding down around midnight, and dedicated night bus routes (NightRider and Nite Owl) discontinued in 2022, late-night patrons rely heavily on costly taxis and private-hire vehicles. 

Using exploratory spatial and network analysis, this project maps the geography of Singapore’s nightlife venues and evaluates how they intersect with late-night public transit availability and passenger movement patterns. Rather than testing causality, the analysis identifies spatial "gap" areas where dense nightlife clusters coincide with weak late-night transit access.

## Research Question
> *Where is Singapore's nightlife concentrated, and to what extent do these areas experience gaps in public transport accessibility during late-night hours?*
